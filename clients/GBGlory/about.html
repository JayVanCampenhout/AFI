One club, built by the people who needed it.

HOW IT STARTED

A comeback that turned into a club.

Green Bay Glory traces back to former WPSL player Kerry Geocaris, who made her own soccer comeback at 39 with the Milwaukee Torrent. When she returned home to play for the United Hurricanes and watched opponents forfeit rather than travel to Green Bay, the gap became impossible to ignore: female players here didn't have anywhere near enough competitive opportunity.

In 2019, Geocaris teamed up with Melissa Cruz-Cuene and Bree Rezachek to found the Glory's women's team in the Women's Premier Soccer League. The team built a following fast — enough to move home games to Capital Credit Union Park, earn a franchise-of-the-year title, and pull in players from as far as Brazil, Kenya, Ireland, and England.

In 2024, Geocaris's son Elliot Bordini — a collegiate soccer player himself — set out to build the same kind of platform for men, launching the Glory's first men's team in the Midwest Premier League. Same mission, same club, a second roster.

OUR MISSION

Advance athletes — on the field and off it.

Green Bay Glory Inc. is the nonprofit behind both teams. We build a lifelong passion for soccer, raise the level of competitive play available in Northeast Wisconsin, and use the sport as a vehicle for mentorship, education, and life-skills training — for young and adult athletes alike.

Every bit of it runs on public support and in-kind donations from this community.

WHAT "GLORY" MEANS

Named for the moment, built for the mark you leave.

The name was chosen for those moments in sport where joy, accomplishment, and unity all show up at once. The club's purple, green, and gold — a nod to Mardi Gras — carry that same sense of celebration into the crest, where a woman and a man are captured mid-kick, back to back, under one sunburst.
