WHY WE EXIST

Built to advance athletes, not just field a team.

Green Bay Glory Inc. is a nonprofit organization that fields both of the Glory's teams. Our mission is to advance opportunities for athletes who want to better their own lives and their community's — through mentorship, education, life-skills training, and a genuine path in the sport, for young and adult athletes alike.

Everything the Glory does is funded by public support and in-kind donations from Green Bay and the surrounding communities.

LEADERSHIP & PERFORMANCE PROGRAM

Glory U coaches the player and the person.

Glory U bridges youth, high-school, and collegiate performance with mentorship, mindset, and leadership development — private and small-group training plus college-recruiting guidance, open to athletes working toward the next level.
