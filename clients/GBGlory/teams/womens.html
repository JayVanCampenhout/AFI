Women's Team

The team that started it all. Founded in 2019 by Kerry Geocaris and Melissa Cruz-Cuene to give top female players in Northeast Wisconsin a real pre-professional path, the Glory women have drawn talent from across the country and around the world.

2025 SEASON

Finished 4th in the Midwest Division.

The Glory women just missed the Lake Michigan Conference playoffs in 2025, continuing a run that has included stints in both the WPSL and USL W League and a roster that has drawn players from Brazil, Kenya, Ireland, and England.
