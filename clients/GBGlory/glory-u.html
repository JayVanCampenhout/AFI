LEADERSHIP & PERFORMANCE PROGRAM

Glory U coaches the player and the person.

Glory U bridges youth, high-school, and collegiate performance — combining elite training, mentorship, mindset, and leadership development to help athletes unlock their full potential as players, students, and people.

Apply now

HOW IT WORKS

Four pillars, one athlete.

On-field training

Private and small-group sessions focused on technical and tactical growth.

Mentorship

Direct access to Glory players and staff who've walked the path already.

Mindset & leadership

Confidence, resilience, and leadership habits that carry off the field.

College recruiting

Guidance for athletes working toward playing at the next level.

WHO IT'S FOR

Every stage, from first touch to college.

Glory U was built to do more than coach the game — it's built to coach the person. Whether an athlete is just discovering the sport or chasing a college roster spot, the program meets them at their stage and pushes toward the next one.
