TRAIN. LEAD. ELEVATE.

Building the player. Developing the person.

Glory U is the mentorship and development branch of Green Bay Glory, bridging youth, high-school, and collegiate performance. We combine elite training, mentorship, mindset, and leadership development so athletes can unlock their potential as players, students, and people.

THE GLORY U EXPERIENCE

Six pillars. One complete athlete.

G

Growth & Grit

Athletes face pressure and build resilience in controlled, intentional training environments.

Mindset · Confidence · Leadership · Accountability

O

Opportunities & Pathways

From highlight reels to college recruiting and career exploration, athletes map their next steps with real support.

Recruiting · College & Career Prep · Goal Setting

L

Learning the Game & Life

In the Glory Lab, players refine technical skill and build the habits of high performance on and off the field.

Technical Skill · Game IQ · Film Study

R

Readiness & Recovery

Mobility, nutrition, and recovery habits used by elite performers — including female-specific health education.

Mobility · Wellness · Female Health

Y

You, the Leader

Self-awareness first. Athletes learn to lead authentically, on their own terms, on and off the field.

Leadership · Emotional Intelligence · Authenticity

U

Unlocking Potential

Purpose-driven coaching that helps athletes see what's possible, often beyond what they see in themselves.

Self-Discovery · Confidence · Growth

WHO IT'S FOR

Every stage, from first touch to college.

Glory U was built to do more than coach the game — it's built to coach the person. Whether an athlete is just discovering the sport or chasing a college roster spot, the program meets them at their stage and pushes toward the next one.
