How an engagement runs

Audit first, then build only what you need

Some clients know exactly what they need — a new website, say — and can start straight from a package. Everyone else starts with the free scorecard, the paid audit, or a quick conversation, so both sides know where the time and money are leaking before anything gets built.

  1. Audit — the free scorecard, the $88 Detailed Business Efficiency Audit, or a deeper review maps exactly where automation will save the most time and generate the most revenue.
  2. Build — a focused first package, scoped from what the audit found: a website package, a digital clone, ad creative, or CRM and automation setup.
  3. Monitor — once your system is live, a monthly licensing and monitoring fee covers the tools it runs on, and we keep watch to make sure it keeps working, so you don't have to babysit it. Each item is listed on its own line, so you always see what you pay for. New changes are quoted as their own project.
